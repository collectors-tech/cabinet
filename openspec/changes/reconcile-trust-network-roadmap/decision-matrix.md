# Trust Network Roadmap Decision Matrix

This matrix is the working decision ledger for #1957. Rows are intentionally
explicit about unresolved decisions so post-beta implementation issues do not
inherit hidden contradictions from the source pack.

| Source area | Canonical rule | Rejected alternative | Unresolved decision | Security/privacy impact | Delivery phase |

| Protocol and technology roles | Signed Cabinet objects are the authority; transports and mirrors only move or verify those objects. | Treating Git history, Radicle identity, DHT entries, CRDT state, or peer transport as authoritative by themselves. | Exact component choice per transport/storage layer after schema approval. | Prevents a mirror or peer session from silently overriding local private state. | 4. Git/Radicle publication; 5. local P2P sessions |
| Public Git/Radicle ledgers and mirror comparison | Public Git/Radicle mirrors may publish selected signed claims or hash manifests only, and each mirror role must be replaceable by GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, self-hosted Git, or Radicle where supported. | Publishing private inventory rows, locations, notes, contacts, or values by default, or making one hosting provider the permanent authority. | Mirror freshness, stale-read warnings, retirement metadata, and mirror-equivalence checks. | Limits privacy leakage while preserving public verification and avoiding provider lock-in. | 4. Git/Radicle publication |
| Signed feedback vaults | Feedback is a signed object with provenance and revocation evidence; deletion does not erase prior signed proof. | Mutable unversioned feedback totals as reputation authority. | Redaction and appeal rules for abusive or legally sensitive feedback. | Keeps auditability without turning feedback logs into secret stores. | 3. signed receipts/feedback |
| Signed trade receipts and QR/manual handoff | Receipts record participant acknowledgements and external consideration notation only. | Escrow, held balances, checkout, stored value, or payment processing inside Cabinet core. | Receipt canonical serialization and participant countersignature flow. | Avoids regulated payment custody and makes transfer evidence portable. | 3. signed receipts/feedback |
| Offline transaction queues and conflicts | Offline events are idempotent signed claims with conflict evidence and manual resolution. | Assuming distributed atomic updates or making one device ledger silently authoritative. | Reservation expiry, double-trade conflict ranking, and merge UI states. | Prevents silent ownership corruption during disconnected trades. | 5. local P2P sessions |
| Direct local P2P exchange | Peer sessions exchange only explicitly selected signed objects or claims. | Ambient access to local database, file system, tools, or private collection metadata. | Platform compatibility for BLE, Wi-Fi Direct, WebRTC, libp2p, and fallback QR/manual modes. | Preserves local-first privacy and constrains peer attack surface. | 5. local P2P sessions |
| Catalogue bundle distribution and Merkle verification | Catalogue bundles are versioned manifests with Merkle/hash verification. | Trusting a transport, hosting provider, or mutable catalogue endpoint without signed verification. | Bundle signing authority, mirror quorum, rollback policy, and manifest compatibility version. | Mitigates catalogue poisoning, stale bundle attacks, and unnoticed rollback. | 6. catalogue distribution |
| User and infrastructure-node identity/registration | User identities and infrastructure-node identities share one canonical key, rotation, recovery, and revocation suite that works without secure-enclave assumptions while keeping role scopes distinct. | Mixing did:key, PGP/GPG/SSH anchors, hardware-only keys, and node operators as interchangeable authority. | Final key type, encoding, recovery, node registration proofs, and compromised-key migration format. | Reduces account/key confusion, prevents node role escalation, and makes revocation testable. | 2. paper prototype/schema; 3. signed proof; 6. network nodes |
| Reputation, validation scoring, and web-of-trust formulas | Identity validation, transaction confidence, and ongoing reputation are separate calculations with bounded ranges and deterministic test vectors. | Combining additive identity checks, feedback reputation, EigenTrust-style propagation, tier tables, and confidence multipliers into one score. | Formula bounds, attenuation, tier thresholds, confidence variables, and propagation caps. | Avoids inflated trust claims, Sybil amplification, and unexplained user-facing scores. | 2. paper prototype/schema |
| Community governance, custodians, revocation, and appeal | Governance decisions must define bootstrap, quorum, custodian scope, revocation, appeal, retirement, and migration before registry code. | A central whitelist that later claims to be community-owned without migration rules or appeal evidence. | Bootstrap authority, quorum model, custodian eligibility, emergency revocation, and appeal evidence format. | Prevents opaque central control, unreviewable community capture, and irreversible mistaken revocation. | 6. governance/network nodes |
| Security threats | Every approved architecture requirement maps to a threat, mitigation, test, and user-visible recovery. | Treating threat modeling as narrative documentation without executable follow-up. | Test-vector format and architecture evidence storage. | Keeps fake peer/store, Sybil/eclipse, tampering, forged/deleted feedback, key compromise, catalogue poisoning, and privacy leakage visible. | 2. paper prototype/schema |
| Paper-prototype trust-failure flows | Connection drop, counterfeit abort, broken signature, stale mirror, feedback mismatch, store attestation, unknown peer, and post-event confirmation must have paper-prototype states before implementation. | Opening production P2P, registry, reputation, or catalogue work before failure handling is observable. | Prototype script format, participant prompts, expected recovery paths, and evidence capture. | Exposes confusing or dangerous trust failures before users rely on the network. | 2. paper prototype/schema |
