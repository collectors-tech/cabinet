## Summary
Surface transient collection/action status messages as toasts instead of easy-to-miss inline text.

## Problem
Messages like "Collection rename cancelled. No changes were made." currently render as inline status text and are easy to miss during live use and review.

## Requested outcome
- use toast notifications for short-lived action/result feedback such as cancel/success/error states where appropriate
- keep the feedback visible enough to notice without permanently cluttering the screen

## Example target
- "Collection rename cancelled. No changes were made."

## Why this matters
- feedback becomes much more noticeable during interactive use
- reduces ambiguity about whether an action/cancel actually registered
- better matches expected app behavior for transient action outcomes

## Suggested acceptance targets
1. Transient action feedback appears as a toast rather than only inline helper text
2. Toast content is readable and visually distinct for success/info/error/cancel outcomes as appropriate
3. The underlying screen remains uncluttered after the toast dismisses
