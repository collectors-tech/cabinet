# Cabinet – Marketing Positioning

## Headline
Cabinet – The Obsidian for Collectors.

## Subheadline
Track your collection. Discover what you're missing. Buy smarter.

## Value Proposition
- Built for serious collectors
- Scanner shows items you don’t already own
- Price tracking over time
- Local-first desktop app
- Bring your own AI (optional)

## Differentiation
Unlike inventory apps, Cabinet actively monitors the market.
Unlike museum software, Cabinet is focused on collectors, not institutions.

## Call to Action
Download the beta and take control of your collection.
